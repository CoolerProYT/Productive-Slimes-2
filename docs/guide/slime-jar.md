# Slime Jar

The Slime Jar picks up a slime so you can carry it, store it or put it in a [Slime Pen](./slime-pen).

<RecipeCard id="slime_jar" />

## Catching

Right-click a productive slime with an empty jar. The jar keeps the slime's **type**, **size** and **name tag** name. The filled jar shows the slime inside in its own color.

<ItemSlot id="productiveslimes:slime_jar" label /> &nbsp; <ItemSlot id="productiveslimes:slime_jar" slime="slime" label /> &nbsp; <ItemSlot id="productiveslimes:slime_jar" slime="gold" label />

The tooltip shows the slime's size.

## Releasing

Right-click a block with a filled jar to let the slime out on top of it. You get the empty jar back.

## Using it in a pen

Right-click a Slime Pen while holding a filled jar to put it straight in, or open the pen and place the jar in its jar slot. Sneak + right-click the pen with an empty hand to take the jar back out.
