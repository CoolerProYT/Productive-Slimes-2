# Slime types

Every slime type in the mod, read directly from its data files. Search by slime, food or product, or filter by tier.

- **Food** is what it eats to grow, to breed, and in the [Slime Pen](./slime-pen) food slot.
- **Squeezes into** is what one of its slimeballs gives in the [Slime Squeezer](./slime-squeezer).
- **Pen time** is one batch in a pen with no food or upgrades. A batch gives one slimeball per slime size.
- **How to get** shows the parents and the catalyst block for [breeding](./breeding).
