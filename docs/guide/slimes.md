# Slimes

Productive slimes are friendly slimes that come in [43 types](./slime-types). Each type has its own color, its own food and its own slimeball. They never attack you and never split when they die.

## Size and growth

Slimes have a size from 1 to 4. Size matters because **a slime makes as many slimeballs as its size**, both in the wild and in a [Slime Pen](./slime-pen).

| Change | How | Food needed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Size 1 → 2 (adult) | Right-click with its food | 4, one at a time |
| Size 2 → 3 | Sneak + right-click with its food | 8 at once |
| Size 3 → 4 | Sneak + right-click with its food | 12 at once |

Each slime only eats its own food. A Stone Slime eats Cobblestone, an Iron Slime eats Raw Iron. You can see every food on the [slime types](./slime-types) page, or hover a slime with Jade installed.

Wild slimes spawn at size 1 or 2. Babies from breeding are always size 1.

::: tip
Growing to size 4 costs 20 food after the slime is an adult, but it quadruples what the slime makes for the rest of its life.
:::

## Feeding an adult

Right-clicking an adult (size 2 or more) with its food, without sneaking, puts it in love for 30 seconds. See [Breeding](./breeding).

## Wild production

Slimes that roam free still drop slimeballs, but at half the speed of a pen: one drop of *size* slimeballs every two pen batches. A wild Green Slime of size 2 drops 2 Green Slimeballs every 2 minutes.

When a slime dies it drops as many slimeballs as its size.
