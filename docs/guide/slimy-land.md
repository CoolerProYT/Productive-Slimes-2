# Slimy Land

The Slimy Land is a new overworld biome. It is where everything starts: wild productive slimes only spawn here, and Slime Crystal Ore only generates here.

## Finding it

The Slimy Land replaces forests in mild climates, so look in temperate areas between plains, birch forests and dark forests. It generates as large round regions. With cheats on, the fastest way is:

```
/locate biome productiveslimes:slimy_land
```

You will recognise it by its bright green grass, sage-colored slimy trees, slime sprouts and boulders made of slime blocks.

## Slime Shrines

Small stone shrines appear in the Slimy Land. Each one has a loot chest and two fenced pens holding a breeding pair of **Coal**, **Snow** (tier 1) or **Copper** (tier 2) slimes. They are a shortcut past the first breeding steps.
