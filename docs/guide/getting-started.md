# Getting started

Productive Slimes turns slimes into a resource farm. You catch slimes in the wild, breed them into rarer types, keep them in pens that collect their slimeballs, and squeeze those slimeballs into real items.

## Install

1. Install [Fabric](https://fabricmc.net/) with Fabric API, or [NeoForge](https://neoforged.net/), for Minecraft **26.3**.
2. Put the Productive Slimes jar in your `mods` folder.
3. Optional: add [JEI](https://modrinth.com/mod/jei) to look up breeding and squeezing in game, and [Jade](https://modrinth.com/mod/jade) to see a slime's size and growth by looking at it.

## The guide book

Every player gets a **Productive Slimes Guide** the first time they join. It covers the same steps as this page in a few short pages, and the mod's advancement tab walks you through them in order. See [Guide book & advancements](./progression).

## The loop

1. **Find the [Slimy Land](./slimy-land).** It is the only place wild productive slimes live, and the only place with Slime Crystal Ore.
2. **Mine Slime Crystal.** Almost every block in the mod needs it.
3. **Catch slimes** with a [Slime Jar](./slime-jar).
4. **Feed and [breed](./breeding) them** to unlock new types, tier by tier.
5. **Put a jarred slime in a [Slime Pen](./slime-pen).** It makes slimeballs on a fixed timer.
6. **Press the slimeballs in a [Slime Squeezer](./slime-squeezer)** to get the resource.

## Your first farm

**1. Find the Slimy Land.** Look for bright green grass, sage-colored trees and slime boulders. If you have cheats on:

```
/locate biome productiveslimes:slimy_land
```

**2. Mine Slime Crystal Ore** in the biome's Slimy Stone. You need a stone pickaxe or better.

**3. Craft Slime Jars.** One recipe gives two.

<RecipeCard id="slime_jar" />

**4. Catch a slime.** Right-click a wild slime with an empty jar. Green, Dirt, Stone, Sand and Oak slimes spawn in the biome.

**5. Craft a Slime Pen** and right-click it with the filled jar.

<RecipeCard id="slime_pen" />

The pen starts making slimeballs right away. A size 1 slime makes 1 slimeball per batch, and a size 4 slime makes 4. Put the slime's food in the pen's food slot: one item keeps it fed for 4 batches at twice the speed.

**6. Craft a Slime Squeezer** and put the slimeballs in it.

<RecipeCard id="slime_squeezer" />

Each slimeball becomes that slime's resource after 4 seconds. A Stone Slimeball gives 2 Stone, an Iron Slimeball gives an Iron Ingot.
