# FAQ

## I can't find any productive slimes

They only spawn in the [Slimy Land](./guide/slimy-land), and only where the light level is above 8. Normal slimes in swamps and slime chunks are vanilla slimes and can't be caught in a jar.

## My two slimes won't breed

Check, in order:

1. Both are **adults** (size 2 or more). Small slimes eat food to grow instead of falling in love.
2. Both were fed **their own** food.
3. Neither is resting. After breeding, a slime waits 5 minutes. Jade shows the time left.
4. The **catalyst block** is within 2 blocks of the parents, if the recipe needs one.
5. The pair actually has a recipe. Two different types with no matching recipe never breed. Check the [breeding table](./guide/breeding#all-breeding-recipes).

## I got the parents' type instead of the one I wanted

Two parents of the same type make a baby of that type when no recipe's catalyst is nearby. Move the catalyst closer: it must be within 2 blocks of the slimes.

## The pen stopped

The message in the pen's top-right corner says why. "Insert a slime jar" means the jar slot is empty. "Output full" means you need to empty the output grid.

## Does the pen need food?

No. Food is optional: one item keeps the slime fed for 4 batches at twice the speed, and the pen keeps working without it. See [Slime Pen › Food](./guide/slime-pen#food-optional).

## Where do I get Slime Crystal?

Mine Slime Crystal Ore in the Slimy Land's Slimy Stone with a stone pickaxe or better. Slime Shrine chests also hold a few.

## Can I automate it?

Yes. Hoppers and pipes work with both machines. See [Automation](./guide/automation).

## I lost my guide book

Craft a new one from a Book and a Slimeball. See [Guide book & advancements](./guide/progression).

## Is this compatible with the old Productive Slimes?

No. This version is a rewrite. Worlds from the old mod won't carry slimes, machines or items over, and DNA, fluids, energy and cables are gone.
